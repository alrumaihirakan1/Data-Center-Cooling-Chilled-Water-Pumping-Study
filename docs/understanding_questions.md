# Six questions to check your understanding

Try answering aloud before opening the separate key.

1. Hand calculation: how much water flow does 1 MW require with a 5 K rise? Use cp = 4188.55 J/(kg K) and density = 999.1 kg/m3.
2. At 40% load, both strategies deliver 68.82 m3/h. How can their gross pump heads be 26.88 m and 9.10 m?
3. Why does one variable-speed pump stop slowing at 66.67% speed, leaving 4.94 m of valve loss at 40% load?
4. At 70% load, what changes when standby P3 is unavailable? How would losing running pump P1 differ?
5. Which operating hours produce the 47.1% annual saving, and what makes the combined unfavourable case use 6.96% more pumping energy?
6. Where could the model misrepresent a real data hall? Give one hydraulic and one thermal example, with the evidence you would seek.
